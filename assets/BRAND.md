**As-of:** 2026-07-14  
**Owner:** 0xNyk  
**Hub:** https://www.nyk.dev/oss/awesome-hermes-agent

## Role

Hermes Agent is the open-source agent maintained by Nous Research. Awesome Hermes Agent is
0xNyk's independent open-source directory. The official Hermes banner leads the README;
supporting graphics borrow its black terminal ground, amber-to-gold block depth, and pixel
geometry while separating official runtime from optional community resources.

## Rules

- Primary: Hermes amber and gold on `#0a0a0a` / `#111`
- Secondary ecosystem signal: `#C5F23F`, kept subordinate to the Hermes palette
- No purple SaaS gradients, no stock mascot art
- The directory is the product: the brand sells **curation + map**, not a chatbot
- Never imply this repository or its maturity tags are official Nous Research endorsements

The banner is copied from
[`NousResearch/hermes-agent/assets/banner.png`](https://github.com/NousResearch/hermes-agent/blob/main/assets/banner.png)
and remains subject to the upstream repository's license and brand ownership.
